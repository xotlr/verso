EIDOLONS: PART I - LYRA
Written by Ronald Bauer

















LOGLINE
When a legendary knight defies orders to abandon refugees during a brutal civil war, he and twelve warriors make their final stand at Thornfield Gate, unaware their choice to die for strangers will echo far beyond the battlefield.
