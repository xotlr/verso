# RABBIT

**A Short Film**

---

FADE IN:

### EXT. RONALD'S APARTMENT - EARLY MORNING

Christmas morning. Still dark.

A window on the second floor.

A PEBBLE hits the glass.

Another.

The window opens. RONALD (22) leans out, squinting.

**RONALD**
What.

ROBERT (22) stands below in hunting gear, rifle over his shoulder, breath fogging. A small silver cross hangs outside his jacket.

**ROBERT**
Merry Christmas.

**RONALD**
It's five in the morning.

**ROBERT**
Five-thirty.

Ronald glances at the cross. Looks away.

**RONALD**
Robert.

**ROBERT**
You said you'd come.

**RONALD**
I said "maybe."

**ROBERT**
That's a yes with anxiety.

**RONALD**
That's a "no" with politeness.

**ROBERT**
You're already awake.

**RONALD**
Because you threw rocks at my window.

**ROBERT**
So you might as well come.

Beat.

**RONALD**
I hate you.

**ROBERT**
I brought you coffee.

He holds up a thermos.

**RONALD**
...What kind?

**ROBERT**
The kind you like. With the oat milk.

**RONALD**
Damn, you remembered the oat milk.

**ROBERT**
I always remember the oat milk.

Long pause.

**RONALD**
Okay, okay. Give me ten minutes.

**ROBERT**
Five.

**RONALD**
Ten.

**ROBERT**
Seven.

**RONALD**
Fine.

The window closes.

Robert waits. Tucks the cross inside his jacket.

Twelve minutes later, Ronald emerges. 

**ROBERT**
(shaking his head)
You're late again.

**RONALD**
Weh, weh, I slipped into a quantum state.

Too many layers. Robert hands him the coffee without comment.

**RONALD (CONT'D)**
I look ridiculous.

**ROBERT**
You look warm.

**RONALD**
I look like I can't dress myself.

**ROBERT**
You can't dress yourself.

**RONALD**
That's mean.

**ROBERT**
It's true.

**RONALD**
Those aren't mutually exclusive.

They start walking.

**ROBERT**
Did you eat?

**RONALD**
It's five in the morning.

Robert pulls a wrapped sandwich from his pocket.

**ROBERT**
Eat this.

**RONALD**
I'm not hungry.

**ROBERT**
You're always hungry. You just don't know it until you're angry.

**RONALD**
I'm not—that's not—

**ROBERT**
Eat the sandwich, Ronald.

Ronald takes the sandwich. Bites.

**RONALD**
...This is good.

**ROBERT**
My mom made it.

**RONALD**
Tell her thank you.

**ROBERT**
Tell her yourself. Christmas dinner.

**RONALD**
I wasn't invited.

**ROBERT**
You're always invited.

**RONALD**
Since when?

**ROBERT**
Since we were twelve.

**RONALD**
That was one time.

**ROBERT**
It was every year.

Beat.

**RONALD**
That was every year?

**ROBERT**
Where did you think you were going?

**RONALD**
I thought it was—I don't know. Accidental.

**ROBERT**
You accidentally came to Christmas dinner ten years in a row?

**RONALD**
I have anxiety.

**ROBERT**
I know.

**RONALD**
I'm not good at—I don't always understand when people actually want me around versus when they're being polite.

**ROBERT**
I know.

**RONALD**
It's a problem.

**ROBERT**
It's fine.

**RONALD**
It's not fine. It's—I know it's exhausting. I know I'm a lot.

Robert stops walking.

**ROBERT**
Hey.

**RONALD**
What?

**ROBERT**
You're my friend.

**RONALD**
I know, but—

**ROBERT**
No. You're my friend. That's it. I don't care if you're a lot. That's what friends do.

Beat.

**RONALD**
...Thank you.

**ROBERT**
You're welcome.

He starts walking again.

**ROBERT (CONT'D)**
Now eat your sandwich.

Ronald eats.

**RONALD**
Doesn't your family do the church thing? Christmas morning?

**ROBERT**
Yeah.

**RONALD**
And you're—

**ROBERT**
I'm here.

**RONALD**
Won't they be upset?

Beat.

**ROBERT**
Probably.

**RONALD**
Why'd you skip it?

**ROBERT**
Because you said maybe.

Ronald looks at him.

**RONALD**
Robert. It's Christmas mass.

**ROBERT**
I know what it is.

**RONALD**
I mean—that's nice. For people who need it.

Robert glances at him. Says nothing.

**RONALD (CONT'D)**
I just mean—why would you skip it? For this?

**ROBERT**
Because this is important too.

He keeps walking.

---

### EXT. AUSTRIAN FOREST - MORNING

Light now. Mist. Snow.

**RONALD**
I have to pee.

**ROBERT**
So pee.

**RONALD**
What if something sees me?

**ROBERT**
Like what?

**RONALD**
A deer.

**ROBERT**
You're worried about a deer seeing your dick.

**RONALD**
The principle.

**ROBERT**
Just go.

Ronald disappears. The sound of pissing. Long.

**ROBERT (CONT'D)**
Jesus.

**RONALD (O.S.)**
It was a lot of coffee.

Ronald reappears.

They walk.

**RONALD (CONT'D)**
So what are we actually looking for?

**ROBERT**
Whatever we find.

**RONALD**
That's ominous.

**ROBERT**
It's nature.

**RONALD**
Nature is ominous. Have you seen nature? Everything eating everything else.

**ROBERT**
That's the circle of life.

**RONALD**
That's a song from a children's movie about trauma.

Robert smiles.

They walk.

Robert stops. Fist up.

Ronald walks into him.

**RONALD (CONT'D)**
You have to—

**ROBERT**
Shh.

A clearing ahead. Snow. Winter light cutting through mist.

A RABBIT sits in the center. White fur against white snow. Perfectly still.

Robert doesn't move.

**ROBERT (CONT'D)**
*(quiet)*
Look at it.

**RONALD**
It's a rabbit.

**ROBERT**
No. Look.

Ronald looks.

The rabbit sits in a column of light. The mist swirls around it but doesn't touch it. Its fur is impossibly white.

**RONALD**
...Huh.

**ROBERT**
Yeah.

**RONALD**
Why isn't it running?

**ROBERT**
I don't know.

The rabbit turns its head. Looks at them.

The forest goes quiet. No birds. No wind.

**RONALD**
Robert.

**ROBERT**
I see it.

**RONALD**
It's looking at us.

Robert doesn't respond.

His hand moves to his chest. Where the cross is.

**RONALD (CONT'D)**
You gonna shoot it or what?

Nothing.

**RONALD (CONT'D)**
Robert?

Robert is staring at the rabbit. Something in his face Ronald doesn't recognize.

**RONALD (CONT'D)**
Hey. You okay?

**ROBERT**
*(distant)*
It's like it was waiting.

**RONALD**
What?

**ROBERT**
Nothing. I—nothing.

He raises his rifle.

His finger on the trigger.

He doesn't fire.

**RONALD**
Robert?

**ROBERT**
I know. I'm—

He breathes.

**RONALD**
What's wrong?

**ROBERT**
I don't know.

**RONALD**
Do you want me to—

**ROBERT**
No. I got it.

He aims again.

**RONALD**
We don't have to—

BANG.

The rabbit falls.

Robert lowers the rifle.

His hands are shaking.

**RONALD (CONT'D)**
...You okay?

**ROBERT**
Yeah. Sorry. I don't know what that was.

They approach.

Robert crouches.

**ROBERT (CONT'D)**
Clean through—

He stops.

**ROBERT (CONT'D)**
No.

CLOSE ON the rabbit.

A BEARD. Long fur around the face, parted in the middle.

Ears draped back like LONG HAIR.

Eyes still open. Gentle. Forgiving.

**RONALD**
Robert.

**ROBERT**
No no no no no.

**RONALD**
It looks like—

**ROBERT**
I know what it looks like.

**RONALD**
Maybe it's pareidolia.

**ROBERT**
NAME ONE OTHER RABBIT WITH A BEARD.

**RONALD**
Maybe it's a mutation.

**ROBERT**
A genetic mutation that makes a rabbit look EXACTLY like—

He can't say it.

**RONALD**
On Christmas.

**ROBERT**
On Christmas.

Robert stands. Steps back.

**RONALD**
It's looking at me.

**ROBERT**
It's dead.

**RONALD**
It's looking at me with forgiveness.

**ROBERT**
*(quiet)*
I know.

**RONALD**
What do we do?

**ROBERT**
*(distant)*
He was right there.

**RONALD**
What?

**ROBERT**
He was right there and I—

He stops.

**ROBERT (CONT'D)**
We burn it.

**RONALD**
You think we can hide this from God?

Robert laughs. Short. Broken.

**ROBERT**
I think God already knows.

---

### EXT. FOREST CLEARING - LATER

A fire in the snow.

The rabbit burns.

Robert sits with his arms around his knees.

Long silence.

**RONALD**
We're going to hell.

Robert doesn't respond.

**RONALD (CONT'D)**
Robert.

**ROBERT**
*(quiet)*
I heard you.

**RONALD**
Are you okay?

**ROBERT**
No.

Silence.

The fire crackles.

**RONALD**
You really believe in all this, don't you?

Robert looks at him.

**RONALD (CONT'D)**
I thought—I don't know. I thought it was just what your family did.

**ROBERT**
It's not.

**RONALD**
All of it? The resurrection. Heaven.

**ROBERT**
All of it.

Silence.

**RONALD**
It smells normal.

**ROBERT**
Don't.

**RONALD**
"This is my body, given for you."

**ROBERT**
We're not eating him.

**RONALD**
We already killed him.

**ROBERT**
I KNOW.

Silence.

**RONALD**
Maybe it would help.

**ROBERT**
Help what?

**RONALD**
"Do this in remembrance of me."

**ROBERT**
That's not what that means.

**RONALD**
How do you know?

Robert doesn't answer.

**RONALD (CONT'D)**
I'm serious. You're the one who believes. Tell me what it means.

**ROBERT**
I don't know what anything means anymore.

Long pause.

The fire is dying down.

The rabbit is cooked now.

**RONALD**
If we do this—

**ROBERT**
I need it to mean something.

**RONALD**
Okay.

**ROBERT**
*(barely audible)*
Forgive me.

---

### EXT. FOREST CLEARING - THE CONSUMPTION

The fire is low.

Robert and Ronald sit across from each other.

Between them, on a flat rock: meat. Just meat now. Unrecognizable.

Neither moves.

**RONALD**
So.

**ROBERT**
So.

Silence.

**RONALD**
Do we say something?

**ROBERT**
Like what?

**RONALD**
I don't know. A prayer?

**ROBERT**
You don't pray.

**RONALD**
I don't know what I do anymore.

Beat.

Robert reaches for the meat.

Stops.

**ROBERT**
I can't.

**RONALD**
Okay.

**ROBERT**
I want to. I think I should. But I can't make my hand—

Ronald reaches over. Takes a piece.

Holds it.

**RONALD**
Together?

Robert looks at him.

**RONALD (CONT'D)**
We do everything else together. We do this together too.

Beat.

Robert reaches. Takes a piece.

They hold the meat.

**ROBERT**
This is insane.

**RONALD**
We're past insane.

**ROBERT**
What if it makes it worse?

**RONALD**
What if it makes it better?

**ROBERT**
What if nothing happens?

**RONALD**
Then we'll know.

Beat.

**RONALD (CONT'D)**
On three?

**ROBERT**
Okay.

**RONALD**
One.

**ROBERT**
Two.

**RONALD**
Three.

They eat.

HOLD on their faces.

Chewing.

The sound of it. Wet. Ordinary.

Ronald swallows first.

Robert is still chewing. Slower.

**RONALD (CONT'D)**
It tastes—

**ROBERT**
Don't.

**RONALD**
It tastes like rabbit.

**ROBERT**
I know.

**RONALD**
Just—regular rabbit. Like any other—

**ROBERT**
I KNOW.

Silence.

They sit with this.

Ronald reaches for another piece.

Robert watches him.

**ROBERT (CONT'D)**
How can you—

**RONALD**
I'm hungry.

**ROBERT**
We're eating GOD and you're HUNGRY?

**RONALD**
I'm always hungry. You said so yourself.

Robert stares at him.

Then something breaks.

A laugh. Small. Involuntary.

**ROBERT**
This is so fucked.

**RONALD**
Yeah.

**ROBERT**
This is the most fucked thing that's ever happened.

**RONALD**
Probably.

Robert laughs again. Then stops.

**ROBERT**
Why doesn't it taste different?

**RONALD**
I don't know.

**ROBERT**
If he was God—if he was really—shouldn't it taste like something? Shouldn't we FEEL something?

**RONALD**
Maybe.

**ROBERT**
But we don't.

**RONALD**
No.

Robert picks up another piece.

Looks at it.

**ROBERT**
Maybe that's the point.

**RONALD**
What do you mean?

**ROBERT**
Maybe he comes back and we kill him. Every time. And nothing changes. And he forgives us anyway.

**RONALD**
Isn't forgiveness good?

**ROBERT**
I used to think so.

He eats.

Chews.

Swallows.

**ROBERT (CONT'D)**
Now I wonder if it's just giving up. Forgiving us because we'll never be better.

**RONALD**
Robert—

**ROBERT**
What if grace is just another word for pity?

Silence.

Ronald puts down the piece he was holding.

**RONALD**
I was so sure.

**ROBERT**
What?

**RONALD**
My whole life. I was so sure none of it was real. I built everything on that. My whole—the way I see things. The way I talk to people. All of it.

Beat.

**RONALD (CONT'D)**
And now I'm sitting in the snow eating God and I don't know what anything means.

**ROBERT**
Welcome to the club.

**RONALD**
It's not funny.

**ROBERT**
I know.

**RONALD**
I thought I was smarter than everyone. I thought I could see through it. And now—

He stops.

**ROBERT**
Now what?

**RONALD**
Now I don't know if I was brave or just— empty. This whole time.

Silence.

They sit with this.

**ROBERT**
You're not empty.

**RONALD**
How do you know?

**ROBERT**
Because you're here.

Ronald looks at him.

**ROBERT (CONT'D)**
You didn't have to come. You never have to come. But you always do.

Beat.

**RONALD**
That's not the same thing.

**ROBERT**
Isn't it?

Ronald doesn't have an answer.

They keep eating.

The fire crackles.

WIDE SHOT.

Two figures. A dying fire. Snow all around.

Eating in silence.

---

### EXT. FOREST CLEARING - LATER

The fire is ash.

Small bones. Picked clean.

Robert hasn't moved.

Ronald wipes his hands on his jacket.

**RONALD**
Feel anything?

Robert shakes his head.

**RONALD (CONT'D)**
Me neither.

Silence.

**RONALD (CONT'D)**
We should go.

---

### INT. ROBERT'S FAMILY HOME - EVENING

Christmas lights. A tree.

Robert and Ronald enter.

**FATHER (O.S.)**
Boys!

ROBERT'S FATHER (55), also ROBERT, appears. His warmth flickers when he sees Robert.

**FATHER (CONT'D)**
You missed mass.

**ROBERT**
I know.

**FATHER**
Your mother was upset.

**ROBERT**
I know, Papa.

Beat. The father looks at Ronald. The warmth returns.

**FATHER**
Ronald. Merry Christmas.

**RONALD**
Merry Christmas, Herr—

**FATHER**
Ten years. Call me Robert.

**RONALD**
That's confusing.

**FATHER**
It's tradition.

He heads toward the dining room.

**FATHER (CONT'D)**
Strange day. Church was packed. Everyone waiting.

He shrugs.

**FATHER (CONT'D)**
Nothing happened.

He looks at Robert.

**FATHER (CONT'D)**
You should have been there.

He exits.

Robert stands very still.

Ronald is staring at the floor.

**RONALD**
*(quiet)*
Nothing happened.

**ROBERT**
Ronald—

**RONALD**
Everyone was waiting. And nothing happened. Because of us.

**ROBERT**
We didn't know.

**RONALD**
I know we didn't know. That's worse.

**MOTHER (O.S.)**
Boys!

**RONALD**
I can't go in there.

**ROBERT**
Then what? Go home?

**RONALD**
I don't know.

**ROBERT**
Stay.

**RONALD**
I can't pretend—

**ROBERT**
You don't have to pretend. Just be here.

**RONALD**
You always do this.

**ROBERT**
Do what?

**RONALD**
Show up.

Silence.

**ROBERT**
I'm weak.

**RONALD**
What?

**ROBERT**
All of it. Church. The prayers. I needed it. To get through.

Beat.

**ROBERT (CONT'D)**
And now I've killed the thing I needed.

Silence.

**RONALD**
I don't care.

**ROBERT**
What?

**RONALD**
That you're weak. I don't care.

**ROBERT**
Why?

**RONALD**
Because you're my friend.

Beat.

**RONALD (CONT'D)**
I thought I was strong because I didn't need anything. Turns out I was just alone.

**ROBERT**
You're not alone.

**RONALD**
I know.

He looks at Robert.

**RONALD (CONT'D)**
That's the only thing I know anymore.

Robert's eyes are wet.

**RONALD (CONT'D)**
If we're going to hell.

**ROBERT**
Probably.

**RONALD**
We do it together.

**ROBERT**
Obviously.

**RONALD**
Promise.

**ROBERT**
I promise.

Ronald opens his arms.

**ROBERT (CONT'D)**
What are you doing?

**RONALD**
Shut up.

They hug.

**ROBERT**
*(muffled)*
Merry Christmas.

**RONALD**
*(muffled)*
Merry Christmas.

They separate.

**RONALD (CONT'D)**
Okay. Goose.

They head toward the dining room.

Ronald goes in.

Robert stops at the door.

From the dining room: his mother saying something. His father's voice. Ronald, already talking too much.

Robert's hand goes to his chest. The cross.

He pulls it out. Doesn't tuck it in.

**MOTHER (O.S.)**
Robert, the goose is getting cold!

**ROBERT**
Coming.

He goes in.

The sound of chairs scraping. His mother fussing. His father pouring wine.

**RONALD (O.S.)**
—and then he fell in the stream, I swear to God—

**ROBERT (O.S.)**
That was ONE time—

Laughter.

Real laughter.

HOLD on the empty hallway.

Christmas lights blinking.

The laughter continues.

FADE TO BLACK.

**THE END**
